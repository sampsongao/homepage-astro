# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Seawide Music Player (“Seawide,” “the app,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), developed by Sampson Gao, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

Seawide is a local music player designed to discover and play audio files stored on your device. We aim to collect only the information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the app.

## 2. Information Accessed and Stored on Your Device

### Music and media library

With your permission, Seawide accesses audio files and associated metadata available through your device’s media library. This may include:

- Track titles
- Artist and album names
- Album artwork
- File locations
- Track duration and other technical audio information

This access is required to scan, organize, and play your local music library.

Seawide does not upload your audio files or music-library contents to our servers. Library information, playback preferences, playlists, equalizer settings, volume-analysis results, and other app settings are stored locally on your device.

You can revoke Seawide’s media permission through your device settings, although doing so may prevent the app from discovering or playing local music.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Seawide uses Google Play and RevenueCat to provide and manage purchases, subscriptions, purchase restoration, and access to paid features.

When you interact with a purchase, these services may process information such as:

- An anonymous app-user identifier
- Product and purchase identifiers
- Subscription or entitlement status
- Purchase, renewal, cancellation, and expiration dates
- App, device, operating-system, and store information
- Diagnostic information related to purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Seawide does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

RevenueCat processes purchase information according to its own privacy policy:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Google processes payment and store information according to the Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

Seawide uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to identify crashes and improve app reliability.

Crashlytics may automatically process:

- Crash reports and stack traces
- App version and installation information
- Device model and operating-system information
- Device or installation identifiers
- App state and technical diagnostic information at the time of a crash

We use this information only for app functionality, debugging, security, and performance improvement. We do not use it for advertising.

Firebase’s data practices are described in Google’s privacy documentation:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 5. Information You Provide

Seawide does not currently require you to create an account.

If you contact us for support or privacy questions, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, and relevant troubleshooting details. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

Please avoid including audio files or other sensitive personal information in support communications unless it is necessary to resolve your issue.

## 6. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with Seawide is used to:

- Scan, organize, and play your local music library
- Remember your settings and preferences
- Provide purchases and paid features
- Restore previous purchases
- Diagnose crashes and technical problems
- Maintain, secure, and improve the app
- Respond to support and privacy requests
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and Seawide does not contain third-party advertising.

Information may be processed by service providers that support essential app functionality, including:

- Google Play for app distribution and payment processing
- RevenueCat for purchases, subscriptions, and entitlement management
- Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, or valid government requests; protect users, the public, or our rights; or investigate fraud, security problems, or misuse.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Music-library metadata, playlists, audio settings, and other locally stored app information remain on your device until you delete them, clear Seawide’s app storage, or uninstall the app.

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations.

Purchase, subscription, and diagnostic information is retained by Google, RevenueCat, and Firebase according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements. Certain transaction records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, tax, or other legal purposes.

Because Seawide does not currently provide user accounts, there is no Seawide account to delete. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information we directly control. We may need to retain limited information when required by law.

Deleting Seawide or clearing its storage does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be managed through Google Play or Seawide’s subscription-management interface.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information associated with Seawide. However, no device, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Google account.

## 10. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where applicable, service providers use legal safeguards for international data transfers.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

Seawide is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by applicable local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how it is processed.

To make a privacy request concerning information directly controlled by us, contact us using the address below. Requests involving Google Play, RevenueCat, or Firebase may also be subject to those providers’ procedures and legal obligations.
